# **General Notes**

* **This section is for those pushing up to the end of R279.** If you’re already done with the game, head to [Endgame Buff Builds]().  
* In Ascension 4, there are almost no buff builds that have to be used while pushing gems and reincarnations to R279, due to the little required setup that each gem prod build has, and this is especially true before R255. However, there are a few buff builds that could prove helpful as you make your climb through each R, so they are listed here.  
* The Ascension will appear very slow at the start until R230. **This is normal, as it is the slowest period of the game.** It picks up quickly at R230, and at R235+ you can get R’s done in as little as 1.5-2h when not going for unlocks.  
* **We use abbreviations for Legacies and Legacy Combos.** For legacies, we use three letters XXY \- the first two are the faction abbreviation, the third is the tier of upgrade. B \= bronze, S \= Silver, G \= Gold, D \= Diamond. For example GBG means Goblin Gold legacy. If the legacy is in parenthesis, buy it with F11750 Relativity, for example (AND). The Legacy Combo we abbreviate as XX/YY, for example AN/GB means the Angel \+ Goblin Legacy Combo.

After e24, you should be running until *at least* a 10x gem gain, but it’s beneficial to run all the way until you start mana wasting (\~1hr) or longer.  
Notable gem ranges:

- e25 slows a lot until e28. One option is to do a much longer run with Undeadline (\~3hrs \- less with more gems \- until you can buy DGAH, which then speeds up other AHs) and should net you around an e3 (1000x) gem gain.  
- e31 is pretty slow as well, this one the only real option is to just wait on faction coins.  
- For long runs, **don’t** buy the Temporal Flux 4 tier upgrade until after you get Dragon Advanced Heritage (it’ll slow you down, and will be much faster after DGAH).

# **Trophy/Unlock Builds**

# **General Notes**

**For the most part, unlocks should not be done the moment they are available \- the time required would be very high for almost no reward:**

- Research: You won’t really have the opportunity to use any research bigger than the 5k A3 researches until R230. It’s also a bit expensive on the faction coins to hit 10000 on each facility (it becomes much cheaper after that) \- so don’t worry if you haven’t even revealed some researches until you get to R230. Early on you can use Reincarnation power to help with faction coins \- Titan (faction) will give D and E facility, Undeadline gives A facility. You can use Faceline or Fairyline for S, Dragonline for F, Druidline for C and W facilities.  
- Artifact Sets: Titan, Undead, Faceless, and Demon are reasonably achievable before R230. The rest don’t bother with until R230+ unless you have a great small value. You may want to save Demon for small value pushing if you don’t plan on using Demons for production.  
- Spell Tiers: Most spell tiers you won’t really be able to even cast at Tier 5 until R225 at least. Before R230, only work on spells you’re using in builds already and don’t require you to spend too much extra time.  
- One thing to think about with spell tiers (now that Call to Arms is achievable before Astral unlock), is getting all to Tier 5 for Arcane Brilliance. While not incredibly important without builds that can take both A13125 and F11000, Arcane Brilliance does provide some max mana, especially in any builds that use AN/FR.

# **General Notes**

* Welcome to the end of the game\!   * Almost every build assumes max gems, L100 for all lineages, all spells tiered to T5, all researches, trophies, and other unlocks, and at least 12h for each alignment except for Neutral. Note that you CAN begin buffing at R255, but it’s best to start at R279.  
  * You may notice the terminology such as “Low-End, Mid-Range, etc etc”. **This is not for gem range, but rather for time spent as each alignment.** Here is a generally accepted dictionary for what these all mean, although some builds may need more or less based on each of their researches required in conjunction to the presence or absence of research point additions from upgrades.  
    * **Low-End:** At least 12h spent in each alignment.  
    * **Mid-Range:** Enough time spent in each alignment to be able to reasonably max out Faceless Set 2, GBP6, DNP6 in a run, and most importantly, a maxed out GB/UD (2-2.5d for order and balance).   
    * **High-End:** About several weeks to a month spent in each alignment. A good metric to follow is if you can afford the High-End variants of Fairies Got Hands, then you are likely approaching the High-End range.  
    * **Extreme-End:** Several months spent as each alignment. The metric for this is if you can afford So You Want More A11625, then you are at the Extreme-End range.  
  * If you cannot find a buff build here at your range, it is likely back in the Progression tab, and will suffice well enough until you can reach something higher up.  
  * When first starting out, besides the simple idea of building up alignment time, it is also strongly suggested to buff Undead faction time for S12250, as this is a common research for High-End and Extreme-End builds. Use Undead Bed to also get S5375 up\!

# **General Notes** 

**In Ascension 4, there are almost no buff builds that have to be used while pushing gems and reincarnations to R279, due to the little required setup that each build has, and this is especially true before R255.** Instead of gems/reincarnations being a determining factor on build classifications (Low End, Mid-Range, High End), the primary factor used in this instance is time spent as a certain alignment to gain research points, which can span into the weeks-months range depending on the build. # **Trophy/Unlock Builds**

For the most part, unlocks should not be done the moment they are available \- the time required would be very high for almost no reward:

- Research: You won’t really have the opportunity to use any research bigger than the 5k A3 researches until R230. It’s also a bit expensive on the faction coins to hit 10000 on each facility (it becomes much cheaper after that) \- so don’t worry if you haven’t even revealed some researches until you get to R230. Early on you can use Reincarnation power to help with faction coins \- Titan (faction) will give D and E facility, Undeadline gives A facility. You can use Faceline or Fairyline for S, Dragonline for F, Druidline for C and W facilities.  
- Artifact Sets: Titan, Undead, Faceless, and Demon are reasonably achievable before R230. The rest don’t bother with until R230+ unless you have a great small value. You may want to save Demon for small value pushing if you don’t plan on using Demons for production.  
- Spell Tiers: Most spell tiers you won’t really be able to even cast at Tier 5 until R225 at least. Before R230, only work on spells you’re using in builds already and don’t require you to spend too much extra time.  
- One thing to think about with spell tiers (now that Call to Arms is achievable before Astral unlock), is getting all to Tier 5 for Arcane Brilliance. While not incredibly important without builds that can take both A13125 and F11000, Arcane Brilliance does provide some max mana, especially in any builds that use AN/FR.

Here’s an example timeline of when to unlock things. If it’s not listed, that means it’s probably not worth the time/effort until a later reincarnation.

**R220-R224**:

- F11250: Excavation requirement met around e30 gems in R224  
- Temporal Flux Tier 5: Can be done during your final production era of R224 (UDTT)  
- Call to Arms Tier 5: Near the end of R220

**R225-R229:**

- Demon Tail (Demon Set 2 artifact), Blood Frenzy Tier 5, Hellfire Blast Tier 5, Maelstrom Tier 5, C11375, D11375, and D11625 (R227+) all pair well together.  
- Lightning Strike Tier 5: Titan, Titan Set, and ELS with a longer era.  
- C11625, E11625, W11375, F11500, F11750: Can be unlocked naturally as you progress.  
- Gem Grinder Tier 5: Unlock at the end of R228 before reincarnating, or push past R gems in R227.  
- W11125: Angeline Titan/Undead with A5375 (quick)  
- S11875: This is now fixed (counts more don’t affect this), unsure of when you will have enough buildings naturally now, but should be doable R235+ easily.

**R230-R234:**

- **Work on remaining Artifact Sets** (also will unlock D11125). Heavenly Drow Mines / Infernal Fae Mines are probably the best builds to push small values, depending on which artifact you have *not* dug up already (just don’t accidentally dig up that faction’s Set 2 until the others are done).  
- Novice Combinator: Can be done with Heavenly Drow Mines at e45+gems, switching the last legacy to anything Titan.  
- A11625: Makersline Elf Merc set, DDB, \~17k excavations (quick)  
- C11875: Elfline Elf Merc Set (quick)  
- F12000, A11125, F12500, E11125,C11125: Should unlock naturally as you progress.  
- W11625,W11875,S11125: Heavenly Drow Mines unlocks these easily (then run Druids/Druidline at least once to cast Grand Balance to complete the W11875 unlock).

**R235-R254:**

- Prestige Artifact Sets (R235): Order of easiness: Drow \> Dragon \> Dwarf, but all are fairly easy.  
- E11375: MKFR Dwarfset (quick)  
- Expert Combinator, E13500: naturally as you progress  
- Spiritual Surge Tier 5: Naturally in R240  
- D13125: unlocked easily with Titan Starter  
- W13125: easy unlock using E5625  
- E13125: Any build that can get AN8  
- Begin focusing on Tier 5 spells each night (collecting activity time), then do the unlock before you Reincarnate the next day. Start with base \+ prestige factions first, leave Limited Wish and Precognition for last (they are the hardest of the Astral spells, so if you happen to climb all the way to Astrals in the meantime it will be much easier). I’d recommend working on spells without builds below first (since they are trivial beyond just getting the activity time). You can try to use the unlock builds before R242, though some adjustments may need to be made (the general idea of each build should still be sound though).  
- Note: If unlocking Limited Wish Tier 5 before Astrals, it is best done just prior to unlocking S13125 (R239+), since you can collect activity time for Catalyst at the same time, which helps that unlock build.

**R242/R244:**  
These are the last research reveals. You *can* stop here for a very long reincarnation to unlock things, but it is highly recommended to just keep climbing to R255, while still working on spell tiers. There are builds below for the unlocks that need some thought. If you’re doing unlocks in R255 you can either use these as a base, or make your own build \- everything will be *much* easier. If doing unlocks *before* R242, just note that climbing beyond reincarnation gems may be needed for some of the builds, but attempt without climbing further first. In either case, you should wait until R255 for the following:

- E11875  
- F13750

**R255:**

- Astral Unlock, push beyond reincarnation gems (lineage levellers work better with all astral upgrades purchased \- stated as e138 gems but may be doable at lower gems). This is fairly quick with Astrals unlocked.  
- Any remaining unlocks. Combine time requirement unlocks with spell tier unlocks. Do this before lineage leveling so the time spent helps out faction coin upgrades (for example, AND).  
- Level all Lineages to L100

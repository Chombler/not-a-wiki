Permanent Upgrades

These upgrades persist through Abdications, Reincarnations, and even hard resets. They are linked to the platform account used to purchase them and that account must be active for them to work.

The Gift bonuses can be turned off in the options (which can be necessary to unlock some Secret Trophies).

Note: All bonuses are multiplicative with each other.

Note: All gifts are multiplicative.

Note: Each gift gives 250% offline production.

Event Sale

During certain Events you will be able to buy "Gift of the Heroes" at 50% off

Gift of the Heroes

Effects: Increase the production of all buildings by 25%.

Effects: Increase clicking reward by 15%.

Effects: Increase Faction Coin chance additively and multiplicatively by 5%.

Effects: Increase offline gains by 250%.

Effects: Provides +2 mana per second.

Effects: Grants +25,000 gold and +20 faction coins of each unlocked faction after every abdication or hard reset. (in other words, you can immediately join the Vanilla Faction of your choice after abdicating.)

Effects: Buying this upgrade unlocks a better upgrade.

Gift of the Kings

Effects: Increases the production of all buildings by 50%.

Effects: Increase clicking reward by 30%.

Effects: Increase Faction Coin chance additively and multiplicatively by 5%.

Effects: Increase offline gains by 250%.

Effects: Provides a special assistant which actually autoclicks twice a second (triggering all real click effects and increasing the click stats) and counts as 2 common assistants while offline.

Effects: Grants 10 Qa (1e16) coins and 500 Faction coins for each faction upon any reset.

Effects: Buying this upgrade will unlock a more powerful upgrade.

Gift of the Gods

Effects: Increase the production of all buildings by 100%.

Effects: Increase clicking reward by 50%.

Effects: Increase Faction Coin chance additively and multiplicatively by 15%.

Effects: Increase offline gains by 250%.

Effects: Increase mana regeneration by 10%.

Effects: Grants 100 Td (1e44) coins and 25000 Faction Coins for each unlocked faction upon any reset.

Gift of the Myths

Effects: Increase all building production by 100%, provide +10 Mana Regeneration per second, multiplicatively increase Mana Regeneration by 25%, and add +1 to Triggered Tax Collections.

Note: This is a permanent account-linked upgrade and can be disabled in Options.

Gift of the Faithful

Effects per day counter: Increase all building production by 10%, assistants multiplicatively by 4%, and Maximum Mana multiplicatively by 2%.

Effects while active: Gain all Scry effects permanently and receive 1 Ruby per day upon logging in.

Day counter: Increases by 1 on a day you log in and decreases by 1 on a day you do not. At the cap, further qualifying days become stacking grace days.

Duration: Each purchase adds 30 days. The duration is unaffected by timewarps.

Rubies

Note: All Ruby upgrades have both an additive and a multiplicative bonus.

Ruby bundle availability and prices vary by platform and are intentionally not listed here. The source-defined Ruby mechanics and thresholds follow.

More Rubies

Ruby Power

Cost: 1 Coin

Unlock Requirements: Find 1 Ruby

Effect: Unlocks the power of Rubies, increasing the production of all buildings by 100% and allowing you to purchase upgrades which increases all aspects of your production.

Ruby Power starts at 100% and increases based on the amount of rubies you have.

Rubies are not affected by soft-resets.

Ruby Assistant

Cost: 1 Ruby (+ 1 for every time this upgrade has been purchased)

Unlock Requirements: Unlocked by Ruby Power

Effect: Additively gain +1 Assistant and multiplicatively increase Assistants by 1% per purchase.

Persists through Abdications and Reincarnations

Ruby Mana Regeneration

Cost: 1 Ruby (+ 1 for every time this upgrade has been purchased)

Unlock Requirements: Unlocked by Ruby Power

Effect: Additively gain +1 Mana Regeneration and multiplicatively increase Mana Regeneration by 1% per purchase.

Persists through Abdications and Reincarnations

Ruby Max Mana

Cost: 1 Ruby (+ 1 for every time this upgrade has been purchased)

Unlock Requirements: Unlocked by Ruby Power

Effect: Additively gain +100 Maximum Mana and multiplicatively increase Maximum Mana by 1% per purchase.

Persists through Abdications and Reincarnations

Ruby Gem Bonus

Cost: 1 Ruby (+ 1 for every time this upgrade has been purchased)

Unlock Requirements: Unlocked by Ruby Power

Effect: Additively and multiplicatively increase Gem production bonus by 1% per purchase.

Persists through Abdications and Reincarnations

Ruby Royal Trading

Cost: 1 Ruby (+ 1 for every time this upgrade has been purchased)

Unlock Requirements: Unlocked by Ruby Power

Effect: Additively and multiplicatively increase Royal Exchange production bonus by 2% per purchase.

Persists through Abdications and Reincarnations

One-Use Upgrades

Timewarp 12 Hours.

Cost: 4 Rubies

Effects: Instantly gain 12 hours worth of production in both coins and faction coins. All timewarps count time as time (this Era), (offline), and (total), allowing trophies and upgrades.

Timewarp 1 Day 12 Hours.

Cost: 8 Rubies

Effects: Instantly gain 36 hours worth of production.

Timewarp 5 Days.

Timewarp 5 Days

Cost: 12 Rubies

Effects: Instantly gain 120 hours worth of production.

Gem Conversion.

Cost: 12 Rubies

Effects: Instantly add your new gems to your current total without Abdicating. (This is nice because you can keep your gold, upgrades, this Era stats, and trophy progress without having to wait for gems.)

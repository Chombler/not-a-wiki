Standard building upgrades increase the production of their associated building. They are available through Ascension 2 and are removed starting in Ascension 3.

Every standard building has 23 upgrades. Hall of Legends also has six bonus upgrades tied to prestige and trophies. Select an icon to jump to its directly readable table row; the icon grid is a visual index, not the only source of information.

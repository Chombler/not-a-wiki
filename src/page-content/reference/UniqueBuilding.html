Unique buildings replace a standard building while you meet the listed faction and progression requirements. The replacement keeps the standard building's count and adds its own production effect.

Research unique buildings

These replacements unlock with the Research system. Each belongs to a faction and requires a specific number of its standard building.

Enchanted Fields

Replaces: Farms

Effect: Production scales with assistants owned.

Formula: (50 × (500 × x)0.5)%, where x is assistants owned.

Requirement: Research and 2,250 Farms.

Elven Training Grounds

Replaces: Warrior Barracks

Effect: Production scales with total clicks made.

Formula: (50 × x0.85 + 50 × ln(1 + x)6)%, where x is total clicks made.

Requirement: Research and 1,750 Warrior Barracks.

Heaven's Domains

Replaces: Heaven's Gates

Effect: Production scales with Mana per second.

Formula: (7 × x0.7)%, where x is Mana per second.

Requirement: Research and 1,500 Heaven's Gates.

Goblin Banks

Replaces: Witch Conclaves

Effect: Adds base production based on Coins owned.

Formula: Adds 2 × ln(1 + x)3 / (1 + 0.1 × (A + 1)), where x is Coins owned and A is Ascension.

Requirement: Research and 1,750 Witch Conclaves.

Undercities

Replaces: Necropolises

Effect: Production scales with time spent in this Era.

Formula: (80 + 80 × x0.8)%, where x is time spent in this Era in seconds.

Requirement: Research and 1,750 Necropolises.

Infernal Realms

Replaces: Hell Portals

Effect: Production scales with trophies unlocked.

Formula: (40 × x)%, where x is trophies unlocked.

Requirement: Research and 1,500 Hell Portals.

Olympian Halls

Replaces: Halls of Legends

Effect: Production scales with Royal Exchanges purchased.

Formula: (6 × x1.2)%, where x is Royal Exchanges purchased.

Requirement: Research and 2,000 Halls of Legends.

Stonehenge Circles

Replaces: Stone Pillars

Effect: Production scales with maximum Mana.

Formula: (30 × x0.6)%, where x is maximum Mana.

Requirement: Research and 2,000 Stone Pillars.

Sunken Cities

Replaces: Labyrinths

Effect: Production scales with the longest Era in this Reincarnation.

Formula: (75 + 7.5 × x0.75)%, where x is the longer of the current Era and longest completed Era this Reincarnation.

Requirement: Research and 2,000 Labyrinths.

Dwarven Forges

Replaces: Blacksmiths

Effect: Production scales with Mana per second.

Formula: (300 × x0.6)%, where x is Mana per second.

Requirement: Research and 3,500 Blacksmiths.

Spider Sanctuaries

Replaces: Dark Temples

Effect: Production scales with offline time this Reincarnation.

Formula: (45 × x0.9)%, where x is offline time this Reincarnation in seconds.

Requirement: Research and 3,000 Dark Temples.

Wyrm Dens

Replaces: Iron Strongholds

Effect: Production scales with Faction Coins found this Era. Offline finds count at half value during offline-gain calculation.

Formula: (0.25 × ln(1 + x)5)%, where x is Faction Coins found this Era.

Requirement: R46+, Research, and 4,500 Iron Strongholds.

Faction Union unique buildings

Beginning in Ascension 2, each faction gains a second unique building through its Faction Union quest. These require a secondary alignment represented by a Proof of Order, Balance, or Chaos.

Swarming Towers

Replaces: Wizard Towers · Alignment: Good, Chaos

Effect: Production scales with active spells; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (2.5 × x2.5)%, where x is active spells.

Requirement: Swarming Tower Quest — own 1,000,000 assistants.

Arboreal Cities

Replaces: Citadels · Alignment: Good, Balance

Effect: Production scales with Faction Coin find chance; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (20 × ln(1 + x)2)%, where x is Faction Coin find chance.

Requirement: Arboreal City Quest — find 1e20 Elven Faction Coins.

Holy Sites

Replaces: Cathedrals · Alignment: Good, Order

Effect: Production scales with Good time this Reincarnation; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (1.8 × x0.9)%, where x is Good time this Reincarnation in seconds.

Requirement: Holy Site Quest — reach 1,000,000 Mana Regeneration.

Slave Markets

Replaces: Slave Pens · Alignment: Evil, Balance

Effect: Production scales with assistants owned; grants Faction Union.

Formula: floor(0.25 × x0.75)%, where x is assistants owned.

Requirement: Slave Market Quest — own 35,000 Neutral buildings.

Flesh Workshops

Replaces: Orcish Arenas · Alignment: Evil, Order

Effect: Production scales with Mana per second; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (35 × x0.65)%, where x is Mana per second.

Requirement: Flesh Workshop Quest — reach 2,000% Offline production bonus.

Burning Abysses

Replaces: Halls of Legends · Alignment: Evil, Chaos

Effect: Production scales with Evil time this Reincarnation; grants Faction Union.

Formula: For each digit of floor(1.25 × x), add digit2 + d / 10%, where x is Evil time this Reincarnation and d is its digit count.

Requirement: Burning Abyss Quest — reach a 3,600-second Call to Arms duration.

Mountain Palaces

Replaces: Deep Mines · Alignment: Neutral, Order

Effect: Production scales with buildings owned; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (0.75 × x0.75)%, where x is buildings owned.

Requirement: Mountain Palace Quest — purchase 4,000 Royal Exchanges.

Ziggurats

Replaces: Ancient Pyramids · Alignment: Neutral, Balance

Effect: Production scales with total Lineage levels; grants Faction Union.

Formula: floor(x1.2)%, where x is total Lineage levels.

Requirement: Ziggurat Quest — own 10,000 Stonehenges.

Forbidden Libraries

Replaces: Monasteries · Alignment: Neutral, Chaos

Effect: Production scales with spells cast this Era; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (30 × (ln(1 + x) / ln(x % 10 + 2))3)%, where x is spells cast this Era.

Requirement: Forbidden Library Quest — reach 5,000 Excavations.

High Bastions

Replaces: Royal Castles · Alignment: Good–Dwarf, any secondary alignment

Effect: Production scales with clicks made this Reincarnation; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (5 × x0.5)%, where x is clicks made this Reincarnation.

Requirement: High Bastion Quest — reset Excavations 3 times this Reincarnation.

Brothels

Replaces: Inns · Alignment: Evil–Drow, any secondary alignment

Effect: Production scales with Royal Exchanges made this Era; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (10 × x1.05)%, where x is Royal Exchanges made this Era.

Requirement: Brothel Quest — cast 1 B (1e9) spells this Era.

Dragon Pastures

Replaces: Farms · Alignment: Neutral–Dragon, any secondary alignment

Effect: Production scales with maximum Mana; grants Faction Union.

Formula: (0.6 × x0.6)%, where x is maximum Mana.

Requirement: Dragon Pasture Quest — have 25 active spells.

Mercenary unique buildings

At R75, Mercenaries replace one building according to their alignment after you reach 125,000 total buildings.

Mercenary Camps

Replaces: Knights Jousts

Effect: Production scales with non-unique buildings owned.

Formula: (1.25 × (1 + x)1.25)%, where x is non-unique buildings owned.

Tyrant Garrisons

Replaces: Evil Fortresses

Effect: Production scales with Offline production bonus.

Formula: (85 + 8.5 × x0.85)%, where x is Offline production bonus.

Freemason's Halls

Replaces: Inns

Effect: Production scales with Faction Coins found this Era.

Formula: (60 + 60 × ln(1 + x)6)%, where x is Faction Coins found this Era.

Elite-faction unique buildings

At R130, each elite faction unlocks one unique building through its faction quest.

Nexuses

Replaces: Wizard Towers, Witch Conclaves, and Alchemist Labs

Effect: Production scales with Order time this Reincarnation.

Formula: (80 × x0.8)%, where x is Order time this Reincarnation in seconds.

Requirement: Nexus Quest — buy 25,000 of the affected buildings.

Pavilions

Replaces: Slave Pens, Warrior Barracks, and Deep Mines

Effect: Production scales with spells cast this Reincarnation.

Formula: (12 × x0.6)%, where x is spells cast this Reincarnation.

Requirement: Pavilion Quest — accumulate 1 day of Chaos-spell activity time.

Halls of Origination

Replaces: Halls of Legends

Effect: Production scales with assistants owned.

Formula: (0.75 × x0.75)%, where x is assistants owned.

Requirement: Hall of Origination Quest — gather 5 TD (5e42) Faction Coins.

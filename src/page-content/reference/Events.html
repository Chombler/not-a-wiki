Realm Grinder events take place during annual festivals such as Valentine, Easter, the Blood War, and Christmas. Participating can award event resources, temporary upgrades, permanent feat rewards, and cumulative bonuses.

Event progress persists through Abdications, Reincarnations, and Ascensions while the event is active. Temporary upgrades expire with their event; permanent feat rewards remain unlocked.

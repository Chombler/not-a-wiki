Dwarven Challenge 1

Enchanted Crystals

"The dwarven lords have asked you to enchant their pickaxes."

Requirements: Angel as Base Faction, Dwarves as Prestige Faction, Reincarnation 6+, Minedwarf upgrade purchased, Elven, Angel and Fairy challenge 1 completed.

Challenge: Cast 750 Tax Collections while having at least 4 active spells.

Effect: Reduce all spells' cost based on the amount of gems you own.

Formula: (-0.05 * ln(1 + x ^ (1 + A / 10)))%, where x is your current gems and A is how many times you ascended.

Upgrade: Good for Angel + Dwarven

Dwarven Challenge 2

Biased Architecture

"The dwarven lords have asked you to provide their people with ale. Hurry up, the dwarves are getting thirsty!"

Requirements: Fairy as Base Faction, Dwarves as Prestige Faction, Reincarnation 11+, Dwarven Bloodline, Elven, Angel and Fairy challenge 2 and Dwarven challenge 1 completed.

Challenge: Build 2,250 Inns within 2 minutes of a new Era.

Effect: Reduce Farms, Inn and Blacksmith cost multipliers.

Effect: Reduces cost multiplier by 0.01; with no other reductions applying, the multiplier will be 1.14 instead of 1.15.

Upgrade: Works with Fairy + Dwarven

Dwarven Challenge 3

Rich Miners

"The dwarven lords have asked you to help them collect trade supplies for all the factions of the realm."

Requirements: Elves as Base Faction, Dwarves as Prestige Faction, Reincarnation 21+, 1750 excavations, Elven, Angel and Fairy challenge 3 and Dwarven challenge 2 completed.

Challenge: Collect at least 100,000 Faction Coins with a single Tax Collection.

Effect: Increase your Faction Coin find chance based on the amount of active spells.

Formula: +(20 * x)%, where x is the amount of active spells.

Effect: Also multiplicatively increase Faction Coin find chance by 150%.

Upgrade: Works with Elven + Dwarven

Dwarven Challenge 4

Paladins

"The dwarven lords are interested in training the troops into pious warriors. Begin the initiation as soon as possible."

Requirements: Any Good as Base Faction, Dwarves as Prestige Faction, Reincarnation 27+, 2000 excavations, Dwarven Challenge 3 completed.

Challenge: Have at least 3,000 Knights Jousts and as many Wizard Towers.

Effect: While Holy Light is active, increase the production of all buildings based on the combined amount of Knights Jousts and Wizard Towers built.

Formula: (5 * x ^ 0.3)%, where x is the combined number of Knights Jousts and Wizard Towers built.

Upgrade: Works with any Good + Dwarven

Dwarven Challenge 5

Underground Foundations

"The dwarven lords have asked you to create underground tunnels connecting the allied factions."

Requirements: Any Good as Base Faction, Dwarves as Prestige Faction, Reincarnation 33+, Dwarven Unique Building, Dwarven Challenge 4 completed.

Challenge: Have 3250 (or 2500 if ascended) Excavations.

Effect: All buildings count 10% more for all purposes.

Upgrade: Works with All

Dwarven Challenge Reward

Iron Grip

Effect: Increase Diamond Pickaxe duration by 8 seconds, and its bonus to Faction Coin find chance is passively applied as a multiplier to excavations Faction Coin rewards.

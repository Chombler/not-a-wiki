Druid Challenge 1

Silent Assistance

"The ancient Druids request evidence that you are a servant of balance before your inclusion to their ranks."

Requirements: Druid as Base Faction, Reincarnation 4+, Halls of Balance upgrade purchased, Elven and Demon challenge 1 completed.

Challenge: Make the building targeted by Grand Balance surpass 99.9% of your production.

Effect: Gain assistants based on time spent in this Era.

Formula: +(3 * x ^ 0.7), where x is time spent in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with Druid

Tip: Click image to see instructions

Druid Challenge 2

Balanced Current

"Our sorcerers are desperately trying to stabilize the balance of the realm, this is your opportunity to gain their favor - and live another day."

Requirements: Druid as Base Faction, Reincarnation 9+, Druid Bloodline, Elven and Demon challenge 2 and Druid challenge 1 completed.

Challenge: Cast Grand Balance 15 times while having exactly 1,500 of each building.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase Mana Regeneration by 1% for every 1,000 buildings built.

Upgrade: Works with Titan

Druid Challenge 3

Eldritch Architecture

"Pleased with your efforts, the elder Druids assigned you with an important task; empower our obelisks, sacred churches and sarcophaguses."

Requirements: Druid as Base Faction, Reincarnation 19+, 1000 excavations, Elven and Demon challenge 3 and Druid challenge 2 completed.

Challenge: Have Grand Balance target Stone Pillars, Monasteries and Ancient Pyramids in that order, for 3 casts of the Grand Balance spell.

Effect: Gain 5 Maximum Mana for every 100 buildings built.

Upgrade: Works with Faceless

Note: The Grand Balance bonus needs to show at least 1% or the targets are invalid.

Note: Since Grand Balance can affect a tier with 0 buildings, you can target the wanted building tier this way:

Step 1: Buy Farms, Inns, Blacksmiths and Deep Mines. Cast Grand Balance.

Step 2: Buy Stone Pillars and Alchemist Labs. Cast Grand Balance.

Step 3: Buy Monasteries, Labyrinths and Iron Strongholds. Cast Grand Balance.

Druid Challenge 4

Overflowing Magic

"The Druids need more spell power, but they are worried about outside influences throwing off their balance."

Requirements: Druid as Base Faction, Reincarnation 25+, Druid Unique Building, Druid challenge 3 completed.

Challenge: Cast 13 Grand Balances in the first 5 minutes of a playthrough with 0 non-Druid researches.

Effect: Increase Non-Unique buildings production based on the duration of your longest spell and buildings' tier. Gives the best bonus to the lowest tier. Does not suffer from Ascension penalties.

Formula: (3 * x ^ 0.7 * (12 - T))%, where x is the duration of your longest spell and T is building tier.

Upgrade: Works with All

Tips: Do not buy Halls of Balance as it increases the spell duration. Make sure you have some excavations to buy the Heritages. (Angel Heritage and Angel 1 challenge both boost your m/s.) To achieve this, you need a min. of 50 m/s with Grand Balance continuously casting during 13 full casts. Reward given at 5 minute mark regardless of when achieved.

Druid Challenge Reward

Primal Balance

Effect: Grand Balance now affects extra buildings based on Mana produced in this Reincarnation.

Formula: floor((x / 100,000) ^ (0.2 / (0.5 * A + 1))), where x is effective Mana Produced in this Reincarnation after “counts more” effects and A is the current Ascension number (0–4).

Effect: Once Primal Balance grants more than 10 additional targets (enough to affect all 11 buildings), each excess target increases the production of all affected buildings. Does not suffer from Ascension penalties.

Formula: (3 * (x - 10) ^ 1.5)%, where x is the amount of additional targets.

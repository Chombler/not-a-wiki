Spells consume Mana and apply their effects while online. Upgrades can change their cost, duration, tiers, activity time, and autocasting behavior.

For tier requirements and tier scaling, see Spell Tiers.

Spell mechanics

TermMeaning
Mana RegenerationMana generated per second, as displayed on the mana orb. Online regeneration stops when the mana pool reaches Maximum Mana.
Maximum ManaThe capacity of the mana pool. It also limits the regeneration value used by offline autocasting mana.
Spell activity timeTotal time a spell has been active. Some upgrades and spell tiers use this statistic.
AutocastingCasts selected spells online when its conditions are met. Offline time is calculated as statistics rather than simulated spell activations.

Offline mana and autocasting

Going offline applies two separate mana calculations:

  1. Mana pool refill: your current mana increases at the displayed offline Mana Regeneration rate until it reaches Maximum Mana. Only the amount that fits in the pool is added to Mana Produced.
  2. Autocasting mana: owned autocasting upgrades credit additional Mana Produced for the entire offline period. This does not add spendable mana to the pool.

Base offline autocasting mana per second: min(Maximum Mana, offline Mana Regeneration) * P / 100, where P is the combined offline mana percentage from owned autocasting upgrades.

Autocasting upgradeOffline Mana ProducedRunning total
Automatic Casting10% of the capped regeneration value10%
Improved Autocasting+15%25%
Masterful Autocasting+35%60%
Planned Autocasting+10%70%
Efficient Autocasting+10%80%
Reverse Autocasting+10%90%
Tiered Autocasting+10%100%

The percentage can be increased further by effects that make offline Mana Produced count more. Spells themselves are not activated offline; qualifying autocasting upgrades separately add spell casts and activity time to the appropriate statistics.

Offline activity time gain (requires Masterful Autocasting): t * min(1, m * d / (n * c ^ 1.5)), where t is offline time, m is offline Mana Regeneration, d is spell duration, c is the tier-one base mana cost, and n is the number of spells set to autocast, excluding Tax Collection.

Cost: 950 Mana - Duration: 20 seconds

Effect: Increase Unique building production based on time spent this Era.

Production Formula: (x ^ 0.825)%, where x is time spent in this Era.

Effect: Also multiplicatively increase Mana Regeneration based on time spent in this Era.

Mana Regeneration Formula: (3.75 * (x / 60) ^ 0.825 * (T + 1))%, where x is time spent in this Era and T is the zero-based spell tier.

Archon Union Effect: Also multiplicatively increase Maximum Mana based on time spent in this Era.

Maximum Mana Formula: (0.375 * (x / 60) ^ 0.825 * (T + 1))%, where x is time spent in this Era and T is the zero-based spell tier.

Spell Tier Effect: Mana produced counts 10% more per spell tier.

An Untimely Death Effect: Also increase Offline production using the Unique Building production formula.

Tier 2 (R120+)

Requirement: Lantern of Guidance (Artifact)

Coin Cost: 120 Notg (1.2e122) Emerald coins

FC Cost: 1 Sp (1e24) Angel, Undead, Dwarven and Drow Coins.

Spell Type: (Chaos) Djinn Faction Spell (R125+)

Cost: 800,000 Mana - Duration: Fixed to 6 seconds

Effect: Provide a random effect based on your chosen base alignment, for 6 seconds. The duration of the spell cannot be modified. Its power increases as you continue casting this spell.

Formula (Production effect): (50 + 5 * ln(1 + x) ^ 1.75 * y)%, where x is Limited Wish activity time in this Era and y is a random number between 1 and Limited Wish casts this Era rolled on cast.

Formula (Non-Production effects): (25 + 2.5 * ln(1 + x) ^ 1.75 * y ^ 0.5 * (T + 1))%, where T is the spell tier.

Note: Limited Wish casts count more based on spell tier: (10 * T ^ 2)%. Its cost decreases by 1% per minute up to 1 hour, then resets.

4: Multiplicatively increase Faction Coin find chance.

Neutral Alignment

2: Multiplicatively increase Maximum Mana.

3: All spells durations count more.

4: Multiplicatively increase Faction Coin find chance.

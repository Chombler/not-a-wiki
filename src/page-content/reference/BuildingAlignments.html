Buildings produce Coins automatically. The first three tiers are available with every alignment; tiers 4–10 use a different building line for Good, Evil and Neutral; and the Hall of Legends is shared by all three alignments.

Production values: The cards show the base production of one building per second, matching the rate displayed by the game. These values are before upgrades and other production bonuses.

Alignment and Proofs of Deed

  • Tiers 1–3: Farms, Inns and Blacksmiths are available without a Proof of Deed and do not change with alignment.
  • Tiers 4–10: the available buildings are determined by the current alignment and require its corresponding Proof of Deed: Good, Evil or Neutrality.
  • Tier 11: the Hall of Legends is available after obtaining any Proof of Deed and is the same for all three alignments.

Hall of Legends

The Hall of Legends does not have a fixed base-production value like the other buildings. Before Hall-specific upgrades, each Hall produces:

250,000 × T Coins per second, where T is the number of Trophies currently counted by the game.

Hall of Fame, Hall of Prestige and Hall of Renown increase the production granted per Trophy. Other upgrades can further change the effective Trophy count or multiply Hall of Legends production.

Building costs

Each building type starts at the first-building cost shown above. That value is its base cost B. Every building already owned raises the price of the next one according to the cost multiplier M.

Cost multiplier

The base cost multiplier is 1.15, meaning that prices normally increase by 15% per building before the game's integer rounding. Applicable flat reductions are subtracted from that increase.

C is the total applicable flat cost-multiplier reduction and A is the current Ascension number:

  • A0 and A4: M = 1.15 − C
  • A1–A3: M = 1 + (0.15 − C) / (5 × A)

Some reductions apply only to particular buildings or building groups.

Exact calculation

The game calculates the cumulative cost at both ends of a purchase and subtracts them. This preserves its integer rounding when buying one or many buildings.

Show the exact cost formula

When n buildings are already owned, the exact cost of buying q more is:

floor(B × (M ^ (n + q) − 1) / (M − 1)) − floor(B × (M ^ n − 1) / (M − 1))

Set q to 1 to calculate the price of the next building. For example, with no cost reductions in A0, the first Farm costs 10 Coins and the second costs 11 Coins.

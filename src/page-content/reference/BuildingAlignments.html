Buildings produce Coins automatically. The first three tiers are available with every alignment; tiers 4–10 use a different building line for Good, Evil and Neutral; and the Hall of Legends is shared by all three alignments.

Alignment and Proofs of Deed

  • Tiers 1–3: Farms, Inns and Blacksmiths are available without a Proof of Deed and do not change with alignment.
  • Tiers 4–10: the available buildings are determined by the current alignment and require its corresponding Proof of Deed: Good, Evil or Neutrality.
  • Tier 11: the Hall of Legends is available after obtaining any Proof of Deed and is the same for all three alignments.

Hall of Legends

The Hall of Legends does not have a fixed base-production value like the other buildings. Before Hall-specific upgrades, each Hall produces:

250,000 × T Coins per second, where T is the number of Trophies currently counted by the game.

Hall of Fame, Hall of Prestige and Hall of Renown increase the production granted per Trophy. Other upgrades can further change the effective Trophy count or multiply Hall of Legends production.

Building costs

Each building type has its own base cost B, shown in the table above. Buying buildings does not change their base production, but each successive building costs more according to that building's cost multiplier M.

Next building

If n buildings of that type are already owned, the next building's cost is the difference between the cumulative cost through building n + 1 and the cumulative cost through building n:

floor(B × (M ^ (n + 1) − 1) / (M − 1)) − floor(B × (M ^ n − 1) / (M − 1))

For example, with no cost reductions in A0, the first Farm costs 10 Coins and the second costs 11 Coins.

Buying several buildings

The exact cost of buying q buildings while already owning n is:

floor(B × (M ^ (n + q) − 1) / (M − 1)) − floor(B × (M ^ n − 1) / (M − 1))

Cost multiplier

The base building cost multiplier is 1.15. Applicable flat cost-multiplier reductions are subtracted first.

  • A0 and A4: M = 1.15 − C
  • A1–A3: M = 1 + (0.15 − C) / (5 × A)

C is the total applicable flat cost-multiplier reduction and A is the current Ascension number. Some reductions apply only to particular buildings or building groups.

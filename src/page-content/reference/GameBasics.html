Realm Grinder is organized around producing coins, purchasing buildings and upgrades, casting spells, affiliating with factions, and resetting to unlock stronger progression systems.

Core loop

  1. Buildings and assistants produce coins.
  2. Mana powers spells that temporarily or permanently improve production and statistics.
  3. Faction affiliation determines available spells, upgrades, buildings, and later progression systems.
  4. Abdication, Reincarnation, and Ascension exchange current progress for persistent bonuses or new mechanics.

Interface and resources

The Game Window reference identifies the major panels and controls. Resources defines the currencies and statistics used throughout the game, while Rubies covers the permanent currency, Ruby Power, and premium purchases in detail.

Progression order

Buildings, factions, challenges, bloodlines, research, lineages, legacies, and Ascension mechanics unlock in a progression sequence. The left navigation follows that sequence so newly relevant systems appear near the point at which players encounter them.

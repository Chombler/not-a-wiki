Makers Challenge 1

Seed of Life

"If you wish to make an apple pie from scratch, you must first invent the universe. And the apple tree, you will have to nurse."

Requirements: Elves as Base Faction, Makers as Astral Faction, Reincarnation 135+, Reality Crater upgrade purchased.

Challenge: Get 10 M (1e7) clicks in this Reincarnation and trigger Elven Luck at least 7777 times in this Era.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase assistants based on the amount of Elven Luck triggered in this Era.

Formula: (1.5 * x ^ 0.85)%, where x is Elven Lucks this Era.

Effect: Gain x8 Faction Coins from any source when they match your Faction, Bloodline or Set.

Formula: *(8 ^ x), where x is number of faction/bloodline/set FC matches (+1 for full FC match, +½ for half FC match) (includes A2950 and Stoneheart).

Upgrade: Works with Elven + Dwarven + Makers

Makers Challenge 2

Artificial Currency

"Extinction is the rule. Survival is the exception, which can only be achieved through subtle deception."

Requirements: Goblin as Base Faction, Makers as Astral Faction, Reincarnation 139+, Makers Bloodline active, Makers challenge 1 completed.

Challenge: Find 1e40 Faction Coins in this Era with at least 3 Excavation Resets in this Reincarnation.

Effect: While Mana Regeneration is higher than Maximum Mana, each time you cast a spell, you also cast free Tax Collections based on the difference between them.

Formula: +(((log10(1 + x)) ^ 2.5 - (log10(1 + y)) ^ 2.5) ^ 1.25), where x is Mana Regeneration, y is Maximum Mana.

Effect: Production bonus from Gems is increased nultiplicatively based on Tax Collections cast in this Era.

Formula: (ln(1 + x) ^ 2)%, where x is number of Tax Collections cast in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with Goblin + Drow + Makers

Makers Challenge 3

Pillars of Creation

"We are like butterflies who flutter for a day and think it is forever. This can be accomplished when you sincerely endeavor."

Requirements: Druid as Base Faction, Makers as Astral Faction, Reincarnation 143+, 25,000 excavations, Druid Set active, Makers challenge 2 completed.

Challenge: Have at least 12 T (1.2e13) Maximum Mana and 30,000 of each building within 5 minutes of a new Era.

Effect: The assistants effect from Infinite Spiral is also increased by Mana Regeneration.

Note: The value of the variable in those formulas is multiplied by your Mana Regeneration.

Effect: Lineage levels count 50% more for the base lineage effect and increase all Advanced Heritage bonuses by 50%.

Upgrade: Works with Druid + Dragon + Makers

Makers Challenge 4

Stoneheart

"We are a way for the cosmos to know itself. Manipulate existence to plant an evocation of yourself."

Requirements: Elves, Goblin or Druid as Base Faction, Makers as Astral Faction, Reincarnation 148+, Makers Unique Building, Makers challenge 3 completed.

Challenge: Without using any Bloodline or Research, reach 30,000 Halls of Origination.

Effect: Pick an additional Set. Increase Faction Coins found matching that Set by 1000%

Upgrade: Works with any Makers combination

Makers Challenge 5

Greatest Invention

"Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known. Sculpt from your boundless imagination and make it your own."

Requirements: Elves, Goblin or Druid as Base Faction, Makers as Astral Faction, Reincarnation 153+, All Balance Lineages at level 45, Makers challenge 4 completed.

Challenge: Have at least 1 T (1e12) assistants, 100 M (1e8) Maximum Mana and 2e20% Faction Coin find chance within 8 minutes of a new Era.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase Maximum Mana based on Faction Coins gained and Mana produced in this Era; spells cast and clicks made in this Reincarnation.

Formula: (0.4 * (0.3 * ln(1 + a) + ln(1 + b) + ln(1 + c) + 4 * ln(1 + d)) ^ 1.4)%, where a is Faction Coins found this Era, b is Mana produced this Era, c is spells cast this Reincarnation, and d is clicks made this Reincarnation.

Upgrade: Works with All

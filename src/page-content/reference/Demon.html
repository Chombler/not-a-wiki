Demon Challenge 1

Trophy Collectors

"The Princes of Hell wish to test your skills at gathering coins without outside help."

Requirements: Demon as Base Faction, Reincarnation 2+, Hellfire Blast upgrade purchased.

Note: Drow can be used but not required.

Challenge: Find at least 2,000 Demon Coins without using Gem Power, Reincarnation Power, or Excavations.

Effect: Increase the production of all buildings and Faction Coin find chance (additively) based on the amount of upgrades purchased. does not suffer from Ascension penalties.

Formula: (x)% and +(x)%, where x is the amount of upgrades purchased.

Upgrade: Works with Demon

Tips 1: Either be very patient, or wait until R7 and use Elves bloodline (recommended).

Tips 2: Scrying for Faction coins, challenges and Ruby Power is okay.

Demon Challenge 2

Legendary Antics

"The Princes of Hell demand a tribute, unleash the Beast by extorting every other faction in this realm. You should perform this task with haste, for they are well known for their impatience"

Requirements: Demon as Base Faction, Reincarnation 8+, Demon Bloodline, Demon challenge 1 completed.

Note: Drow can be used but not required.

Challenge: Have exactly 666 Royal Exchanges across all factions, with 0 Demon Exchanges, in under 666 seconds current playtime.

Effect: Increase Maximum Mana by 6.66% of your number of Halls of Legends.

Upgrade: Works with Fairy

Tip: High gem and low Excavations (relative). You will need 95 Royal Exchanges of 6 Factions and 96 Royal exchanges of the 7th Faction, Don't buy Demon exchanges. Drow's blackmail upgrade helps the challenge, and the exchanges don't have to be spread evenly.

Demon Challenge 3

Passage to Tartarus

"The Demon King has ordered you to prove your might to the entire realm."

Requirements: Demon as Base Faction, Reincarnation 18+, Demon Unique Building, Demon challenge 2 completed.

Note: Drow can NOT be used unless you can get Drow's Unique Building at R28+.

Challenge: Own at least 2,500 Halls of Legends and 333 trophies.

Effect: Increase the production of Non-Unique buildings based on the amount of unlocked trophies. does not suffer from Ascension penalties.

Formula: (0.8 * x ^ 0.8)%, where x is the amount of unlocked trophies.

Upgrade: Works with All

Tip: Use Goblin Bloodline, can be easily done around Uvg(e66) gems.

Demon Challenge Reward

Hellfire Burst

Effect: Hellfire Blast also counts Evil spells cast in this reincarnation to increase its bonus.

Formula: (0.1 * x), where x is evil spells cast in this reincarnation.

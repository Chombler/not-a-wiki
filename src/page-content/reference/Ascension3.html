Ascension 3 begins after ascending at R159 and covers R160 through R219. It replaces Emerald Coins with Amethyst Coins and substantially restructures Research, Mercenaries, building upgrades, and late-game faction access.

What changes

  • Emerald Coins are replaced by Amethyst Coins.
  • Coin-cost upgrades from previous currencies become free.
  • Lineages above level 50 are reduced to level 50.
  • All Researches with a research number below 405 become inaccessible.
  • Research changes from slots to a Research Budget. A research costs a number of budget points equal to its research number.
  • The normal quantity-based building upgrade series is removed starting in Ascension 3.
  • Spell Tier 4 becomes the maximum ordinary spell tier available in this Ascension.

Research Budget

Ascension 3 starts with no Research Budget. Budget is supplied by Ascension-era effects, including the R170 power, Research Facilities, the Archon Bloodline, and other later upgrades. See Research for the complete budget rules and Research Facilities for facility formulas.

Factions and Mercenaries

Ascension 3 introduces the Astral factions: Archon, Djinn, and Makers. Mercenary affiliation must be unlocked for Ascension 3 through Mercenary Duel; see the Mercenary reference for its requirements and A3 rules.

Systems retained until Ascension 4

Assistant, alignment, Wall Fragment, Gem Potency, and Sun Force upgrade series remain available in Ascension 3. They are removed when entering Ascension 4.

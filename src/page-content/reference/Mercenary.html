Mercenary Challenge 1

Unlimited Knowledge

"To the many, having unlimited knowledge would mean having unlimited power. To the few, it is only some of that knowledge that has unlimited potential."

Requirements: R190, Order Mercenary, Chrono Loading, Nexus Unique Building, and 20,000 Research Points used.

Challenge: Have at least 1e20 Mana Regeneration without using any Chaos or Balance upgrade or effect except Challenges and Royal Exchanges.

Effect: Gain the Base, Prestige, and Astral Bloodlines for your alignments.

Tip: Unaligned, Mercenary, Prestige, and Order faction-related upgrades are allowed. Upgrade alignment is determined by the background color of the upgrade.

In Game: Click the challenge to view the Bloodline container

Upgrade: Works with Order Mercenary

Mercenary Challenge 2

The Source of Magic

"The Realm may have fallen to chaos, but there are still contracts that must be completed. What better way to complete those contracts than to embrace madness itself."

Requirements: R194, Chaos Mercenary, Two Chaos Bloodlines, 4 Chaos spells.

Challenge: Have at least 65 spell tiers active within 5 minutes of a new Era.

Effect: Gain the Chaos Madness spell.

Chaos Madness

Cost: 500,000 Mana - Duration: Fixed to 20 seconds

Effect: Grants the effect of a random lineage, with the level of your highest lineage for 20 seconds.

Tip: Lineage level from spell is affected by lineage modifiers

Upgrade: Works with Chaos Mercenary

Mercenary Challenge 3

Men of the World

"The best armies in the realm are those that are well-rounded: able to take on any kind of enemy that approaches."

Requirements: R198, Balance Mercenary, 40,000 deepest Excavations made in this Reincarnation, Mercenary Set.

Challenge: Have assistants, Maximum Mana, Mana Regeneration and Faction Coin chance fall within the same order of magnitude while above 1e12.

Effect: Gain the Base and Prestige Sets for your alignments.

In Game: Click the challenge to view the Set container

Upgrade: Works with Balance Mercenary

Mercenary Challenge 4

Return of the King

"The Mercenary Lord has returned, with newfound knowledge and power. Another challenge awaits you..."

Requirements: R202, Any Mercenary, All lineages level 75.

Challenge: Have exactly one assistant and reach a total of 1e40 Faction Coins within 10 minutes of a new Era.

Effect: You can pick one additional Union Power.

Upgrade: Works with Any Mercenary

Mercenary Challenge 5

The Art of War

"An expert Mercenary should learn by other cultures. That has been our call for generations. Now, it is your turn to answer that call."

Requirements: R206, Any Mercenary, 15,000 Royal Exchanges made in this Era, at least one upgrade from each of 15 different factions, and at least 1 day spent with every alignment in this Reincarnation.

Challenge: Have at least 500,000 buildings without using any research.

Effect: Unique Buildings that match your Lineage and Set Factions or their Faction Coin affinity have their production increased based on the amount of time spent with each of those factions. Does not suffer from Ascension penalties.

Formula: (x ^ 0.7)%, where x is time spent with the respective faction.

Note: Works similarly to R58 and R115 powers, so a Fairy Unique building will also earn half a bonus from Djinn faction.

Upgrade: Works with All

Mercenary Challenge Reward

Warcry

Call to Arms gains additional effects based on your alignments, scaling off your Call to Arms strength.

General Formula: y = 25 + (0.3 * x) ^ 0.975, where x is building count.

Good: Call to Arms gains 1 tier. Tax Collection casts in this Era are added as a base for its bonus.

Formula: (1.5 * x ^ 0.5)%, where x is Tax Collection casts this Era.

Evil: Multiplicatively increase Tax Collection worth in seconds.

Formula: (y ^ 0.35)%

Neutral: Multiplicatively increase Faction Coins find chance.

Formula: (0.35 * y ^ 0.35)

Order: Autoclick floor(1.25 * ln(1 + y) ^ 1.25) times per second and increase all spell durations by 500%.

Chaos: Multiplicatively increase assistants (Temporary).

Formula: (0.35 * y ^ 0.35)

Balance: Increases Royal Exchange Bonus multiplicatively and makes them count more.

Formula: (0.45 * y ^ 0.45)

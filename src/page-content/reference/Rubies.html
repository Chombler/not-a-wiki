In-game description

Rubies are a rare resource that increase multiple parts of production. Collecting them unlocks permanent bonuses, upgrades and trophies, while owned Rubies can be spent on additional perks.

The game tracks currently owned Rubies separately from all-time collected Rubies. Spending Rubies reduces the owned balance, but it does not reduce the all-time total used by Ruby Power tiers. Rubies and purchased Ruby bonuses persist through Abdications and Reincarnations.

Getting Rubies

  • Excavation: free Rubies are awarded the first time specific excavation thresholds are reached. Each successive Ruby requires more excavations.
  • Seasonal events: event rewards and daily presents may award Rubies.
  • Royal Shop: Rubies may be purchased without a quantity limit.

Finding the 1st, 10th, 25th and 50th excavation Ruby also awards a trophy.

The excavation threshold for Ruby n is 25 × n × (n + 1), where n is the number of Rubies found through excavation.

Spending Rubies

After the first Ruby unlocks Ruby Power, owned Rubies can be allocated among five permanent upgrades. If an upgrade has been purchased x times, its next purchase costs x + 1 Rubies.

Ruby upgradeEffect per purchase
Ruby Assistant+1 assistant
Ruby Mana Regeneration+1 Mana per second
Ruby Max Mana+100 Maximum Mana
Ruby Gem Bonus+1% Gem production bonus
Ruby Royal Trading+2% Royal Exchange production bonus

Rubies can also pay for Excavation Resets, Gem conversion without Abdicating, and resource offers during seasonal events.

Ruby Power tiers

Ruby Power unlocks after collecting the first Ruby and begins with +100% production to all buildings. Its tier is based on all-time collected Rubies, so spending Rubies does not reduce this production bonus.

Ruby appearance

Rubies have one standard appearance. Event cosmetic rewards change Mana colors or ordinary coin-particle shapes, not Rubies; the Ruby's appearance does not change its value or mechanics.

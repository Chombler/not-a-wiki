Spell Tiers let a spell cast at several tiers at once. This page covers how tiers scale, how their upgrades are bought, and the requirements for permanently unlocking each upgrade. See Spells for the underlying spell effects.

How spell tiers work

From R42+, Spell Tiers become available. A spell's purchased tier is the number of simultaneous casts and normally multiplies its production effect once per cast.

Before Ascension penalties, a production multiplier B cast at tier T normally becomes B ^ T. Gem Grinder scales linearly instead, while Dragon's Breath adds another breath color. Peripheral effects such as Fairy Chanting's assistant multiplier are not repeated by each simultaneous cast.

Ordinary spells unlock at most one additional tier per Ascension: Tier 1 in Ascension 0, Tier 2 in Ascension 1, Tier 3 in Ascension 2, and so on. Tax Collection with Round Table, Dragon's Breath, Catalyst, Twisting Nether, and seasonal spells use their own rules.

Each simultaneous cast is calculated separately and receives its own Ascension penalty. Let A be the current Ascension and t the zero-based tier-upgrade count (displayed tier minus one). A spell with no tier upgrade uses exponent 0.1 / A. A purchased tier uses exponent (0.5 / A) × 0.5 ^ ((t > A ? 0.5 : 1) × (A - t)). The resulting tier effects multiply together.

Dragon's Breath starts with five colors and gains colors across Ascensions. Its production effects do not suffer Ascension penalties.

Unlocking and buying tiers

Each Spell Tier upgrade must be unlocked once, after which the unlock is permanent. The upgrade itself must be purchased again after every Abdication. Every purchase has a Faction Coin cost.

The ordinary coin cost advances with the Ascension that introduces the tier: Tier 2 costs Diamond Coins in A1, Tier 3 costs Emerald Coins in A2, Tier 4 costs Amethyst Coins in A3, and Tier 5 costs Sapphire Coins in A4. A tier's ordinary coin cost becomes free in later Ascensions. Spells introduced in A2 therefore receive Tier 2 free, then follow the same currency sequence from Tier 3 onward.

Autocasting and shared tier bonuses

Tiered Autocasting is not required to unlock or purchase Spell Tier upgrades. Its upgrade becomes available in Ascension 1 at R42 after purchasing Hatch! and Efficient Autocasting. Efficient Autocasting requires 50 M Mana Produced this Reincarnation. Purchasing the Tiered Autocasting upgrade grants its trophy.

Tiered Autocasting will automatically set the autocasted spell tier at the highest you have bought, but you can change it to a lower tier if you wish.

Reverse Autocasting and Tiered Autocasting are separate, independent upgrades; Tiered Autocasting does not require Reverse Autocasting.

Additional Effects: Each tier upgrade has a spell-specific effect and, except for Dragon's Breath, also increases offline production based on Mana statistics. Each purchased upgrade adds 200% to that spell's offline Faction Coin multiplier; upgrades belonging to the same spell add together, while the multipliers from different spells multiply.

Raw offline-production formula per tier upgrade: (m ^ (2 / max(3, A)) + r ^ (2 / max(3, A))) ^ 0.4%, where m is Maximum Mana, r is Mana Regeneration per second, and A is the current Ascension. Each upgrade's result is then subjected to the Ascension penalty assigned to that tier; the in-game tooltip shows the combined purchased bonus.

Note: Offline bonuses from tier upgrades are assigned to the Ascension in which that tier becomes available. Dragon's Breath tier upgrades do not provide these offline bonuses.

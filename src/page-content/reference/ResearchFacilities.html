Research Facilities hold upgrades for seven branches. Use the Research List for complete branch entries or the Research Tree for the interactive layout.

Spellcraft: Fairy, Faceless, and Drow affinity.

Craftsmanship: Elven, Titan, and Dwarven affinity.

Divine: Angel, Faceless, and Dwarven affinity.

Economics: Goblin, Druid, and Dwarven affinity.

Alchemy: Undead, Druid, and Drow affinity.

Warfare: Demon, Titan, and Drow affinity.

Forbidden: Mercenary affinity; available after unlocking the Apeiron upgrade.

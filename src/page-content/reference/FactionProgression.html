Several progression systems extend the powers of factions without replacing ordinary faction affiliation. They unlock at different stages and use different kinds of faction history.

SystemRoleReference
BloodlinesBorrow a faction-specific bloodline effect while playing eligible factions.Bloodlines
HeritagesPermanent faction rewards purchased with faction coins after earning the corresponding trophies.Heritages
LineagesLevelled faction powers that consume lineage resources and gain perks at specified levels.Lineages
LegaciesLate-game faction combinations that join effects from multiple faction histories.Legacies

Relationship to affiliation

These systems use faction identity, but they are separate from the trade treaty, friendship pact, alliance, and union upgrades described on the Factions page. Each detail page records its own unlocks, availability, persistence, and effects.

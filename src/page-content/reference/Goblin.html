Goblin Challenge 1

Discount Season

"The goblin king has revealed the secrets of his race's success. Patience and persistence."

Requirements: Goblin as Base Faction, Reincarnation 2+, Greed Drive upgrade purchased.

Note: Drow can be used but not required.

Challenge: Have Green Fingers Discount roll more than 1,000 as its random base value.

Effect: Green Fingers Discount triggers every 30 seconds and casts 10 Tax Collections upon triggering.

Note: Tax Collections cast this way will not trigger upgrades such as GB7.

Note: While Green Fingers Discount says it works offline, it does not work in the same way as online, and this challenge cannot be completed offline or when coming online based on the offline effect of Green Fingers Discount.

Note: There's a 60% chance of getting it every time Green Fingers Discount triggers.

Upgrade: Works with Goblin

Goblin Challenge 2

Elite Warriors

"Goblin elites are used to unimaginable wealth. They will work for you after you bribe them generously and show them your worth."

Requirements: Goblin as Base Faction, Reincarnation 8+, Goblin Bloodline, Goblin challenge 1 completed.

Note: Drow can be used but not required.

Challenge: Purchase at least 250 upgrades and 15,000 buildings.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase production bonus from Gems based on Tax Collection casts in this Era.

Formula: (2 * ln(1 + x) ^ 2)%, where x is Tax Collection casts in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with Demon

Goblin Challenge 3

Smuggled Jewels

"The goblin king wants a castle made of jewels all to himself."

Requirements: Goblin as Base Faction, Reincarnation 18+, Goblin Unique Building, Goblin challenge 2 completed.

Note: Drow can NOT be used unless you can get Drow's Unique Building at R28+.

Challenge: Have at least 1 Qavg (1e75) gems in the abdication treasury while using Goblin Bloodline (post Ascension: 1 B (1e9) gems).

Effect: Each spell cast also casts a free Tax Collection, and triggered Tax Collections are multiplicatively increased by 25%.

Upgrade: Works with All

Goblin Challenge Reward

Avarice Drive

Effect: Goblin's Greed counts Faction Coins found in this Reincarnation.

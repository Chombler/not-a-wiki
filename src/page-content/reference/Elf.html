Elven Challenge 1

Advanced Civilization

"The elven council have asked you to provide proof of your loyalty to their cause. Hopefully you'll be quick and efficient enough to impress them."

Requirements: Elves as Base Faction, Reincarnation 2+, Sun Blessing upgrade purchased.

Note: Dwarven can be used but not required.

Challenge: Find at least 100,000 Elven Coins within 1 hour of a new Era.

Effect: Reduces Elven Exchange cost multiplier.

Effect: Reduces cost multiplier by 0.025; with no other reductions applying, the multiplier will be 1.075 instead of 1.1.

Effect: Clicks count more based on Elven Luck triggered in this Era.

Formula: (10 + x ^ 0.5)%, where x is Elven Luck triggered in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with Elven

Tip: Don't forget to use excavations.

Elven Challenge 2

Light Collector

"The elven council has asked you to gather the light of countless stars. Time is of the essence!"

Requirements: Elves as Base Faction, Reincarnation 8+, Elven Bloodline, Elven challenge 1 completed.

Note: Dwarven can be used but not required.

Challenge: Gather 60,000 automatic clicks in less than 2 hours.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase Mana Regeneration based on clicks made in this Era, and autoclick once per second.

Formula: (x ^ 0.25)%, where x is clicks made in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with Angel

Tip: Buy all upgrades and auto cast Moon Blessing only. Takes about 1 hour 25 minutes.

Elf Challenge 3

Runic Conversion

"The elven council intends to use the light you've gathered as a weapon against their corrupt cousins, as soon as they detect weakness."

Requirements: Elves as Base Faction, Reincarnation 18+, Elven Unique Building, Elven challenge 2 completed.

Note: Dwarven can NOT be used unless you can get Dwarven's Unique Building at R28+.

Challenge: Buy at least 200 of each Royal Exchange while having 0 Drow Exchanges.

Effect: Multiplicatively increase Faction Coin find chance based on clicks made in this Era.

Formula: (x ^ 0.25)%, where x is clicks made in this Era.

Upgrade: Works with all

Elven Challenge Reward

Star Blessing

Effect: While Moon Blessing is active, progressively increase the Elven Luck triggered statistic each second.

Formula: +floor(1 + t ^ 0.2) per second, where t is Moon Blessing's elapsed duration.

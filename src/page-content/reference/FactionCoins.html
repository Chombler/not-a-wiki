Faction Coins are eight faction-specific currencies used for faction progression. They are distinct from the main coin currency and are shown in the Upgrades panel.

Neutral factions

Titan, Druid and Faceless do not have dedicated Faction Coin currencies. Each Neutral faction instead uses one Good and one Evil vanilla currency for its Trade Treaty, Friendship Pact and Alliance.

Before Ascension 4, the Trade Treaty costs 250 of each listed currency, the Friendship Pact costs 2,500 of each, and the Alliance costs 10,000 of each. Ascension 4 changes faction purchase costs; the individual faction pages list the current costs and requirements.

Finding Faction Coins

Faction Coin find chance determines how often normal find attempts award Faction Coins. Clicking, assistants, excavations, spells and upgrades can supply or modify find attempts and rewards. Effects may add to the displayed chance, multiply it, or grant Faction Coins directly; use the wording of each effect to distinguish them.

Faction Coins found are tracked in several time scopes because many upgrades use those statistics. The current balance is separate from statistics such as Faction Coins found in this game, this Reincarnation or all time.

Spending Faction Coins

Faction Coins are typed currencies, not one shared balance. A purchase may require one specific type, a faction's paired types, or every type; its description lists which balances are spent.

  • Faction affiliation and tier unlocks: Trade Treaties, Friendship Pacts, Alliances and later Unions use the faction's currency or currency pair to open its spell and successive upgrade tiers.
  • Royal Exchanges: Each exchange spends one Faction Coin type to increase all-building production and contribute to the Royal Market bonus. Repeated purchases of that exchange become more expensive.
  • Heritages: Unlocked Heritages use their matching currency or currency pair; the Mercenary Heritage uses every type.
  • Spell tier upgrades: Each tier lists the Faction Coin types and amount required to strengthen its spell.
  • Research: Research Facilities and Research Points use currencies associated with their factions and branches.
  • Lineage levels: Each level spends the currency associated with that Lineage and scales its level-based effects.
  • Mercenary contracts and upgrades: Mercenary affiliation and selected upgrades use the applicable faction currencies.
  • Legacies: Legacy Containers and Legacy Combos use specified base or Prestige Faction Coins.

Some individual unlock upgrades and other purchases also have Faction Coin costs. Their own reference entries give the required types and current amounts.

Faction Coins and resets

The current Faction Coin balances reset on Abdication, Reincarnation and Ascension. Longer-term Faction Coin statistics follow the time scope in their names and the rules of the reset being performed.

Faction Coin appearance

Each currency uses its own icon in the interface and its own particle when found, making the eight balances visually distinct. These appearances identify the Faction Coin type; they do not change its value or mechanics.

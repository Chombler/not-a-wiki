Ascension 1 begins after ascending at R39 and covers R40 through R99. It changes the main currency and applies the first Ascension scaling reduction while retaining the core faction, challenge, bloodline, and Research systems introduced before it.

Entering Ascension 1

AscensionAscend at R39; continue at R40
Reincarnation rangeR40–R99
Main currencyDiamond Coins
Gem requirement1e27 × 100(R − 40)
Gems to coinsn × (n + 1) × 5e11, where n is Gems

What changes

  • Gold Coins are replaced by Diamond Coins.
  • Coin-cost upgrades from the previous currency become free.
  • Applicable production, clicking reward, and assistant-production bonuses receive one Ascension Penalty.
  • Building and excavation cost multipliers are reduced for the new Ascension.
  • Spell Tier 2 becomes the maximum ordinary spell tier available in this Ascension.

Ascension Penalty

The first Ascension converts an applicable percentage bonus using:

Formula: (((x / 100 + 1)0.1 − 1) × 100)%, where x is the original percentage bonus.

Bonuses which do not increase building production, clicking reward, or assistant production are not reduced by the general Ascension Penalty. Gifts, Scry rewards, Ruby bonuses, Event bonuses, and Gem bonuses are also unaffected.

Systems available during Ascension 1

  • Spell tiers expand to Tier 2.
  • Lineages become available during this Ascension.
  • The Dragon faction and its related systems become available during this Ascension.
  • Research continues to use facility slots rather than the later Research Budget system.

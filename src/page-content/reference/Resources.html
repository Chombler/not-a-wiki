Realm Grinder uses five primary resources. Each has a different role and persists through a different part of the reset cycle.

Resource summary

ResourceHow it is gainedWhat it is used forReset behavior
ManaGenerated continuously by Mana Regeneration.Casting spells.The current pool and purchased bonuses reset when a new Era begins.
CoinsPrimarily produced by buildings, assistants and clicks.Buildings, upgrades and other purchases.Reset on Abdication and every larger reset.
GemsAwarded when abdicating, based on the coins represented by the new total.Increasing production and meeting progression requirements.Kept through Abdication; reset on Reincarnation and Ascension.
Faction CoinsFound through faction-coin find chance and other effects.Faction upgrades, Royal Exchanges, heritages and other faction purchases.Reset on Abdication and every larger reset.
RubiesExcavations, events and purchases.Ruby Powers and certain special purchases.Persist through Abdication, Reincarnation and Ascension.

Other currencies

Temporary event currencies are documented with their respective events. Kreds are Kongregate's platform currency and are documented with premium upgrades.

Cosmetic appearances

Some event feats permanently unlock alternate mana colors or coin-particle shapes. These are cosmetic settings only: they do not create a different resource or change its value, production or reset behavior. This reference uses the standard resource icons unless a cosmetic reward itself is being documented.

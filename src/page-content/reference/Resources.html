Resources used in Realm Grinder:

Mana: A magic liquid necessary to cast spells.

A new Era starts with 500 current Mana and 1,000 base Maximum Mana.

Currencies

Coins: Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Amethyst or Sapphire, depending on which Ascension you are on.

Faction Coins: of eight different types, each related to a faction.

Gems: gained when soft-resetting, abdicating.

Rubies: enable Ruby Power and can also be spent on certain premium upgrades.

Kreds: Kongregate's platform currency, available only on the Kongregate version.

Coins, Gems and Rubies are visible in the bottom menubar. Faction Coins are displayed at the bottom of the Upgrades tab.
